The hardest tables in Austin — and how to actually get them

26 near-impossible reservations and 11 more beloved rooms, each vetted against real critics. We watch the ones that never have availability and alert you the second a table for two opens up.

Get free alerts for Austin →

Every spot below is endorsed by a named critic or publication — never Yelp, Google reviews, or SEO listicles.

  • Tsuke Edomae

    ●●●●●
    Mueller · Japanese Omakase (Edomae sushi) · $$$$ · Tock

    Called the single toughest reservation in Austin: only 8 seats and limited weekly services; tickets drop just twice a year (a Sunday at 9am) in months-long blocks and regulars soak up most of the ~50 seats a month.

    Vetted by The Infatuation
    Book on Tock
